# Nigeria Electricity Sector Performance Analytics (2025)

## Project Overview

This project analyses the financial and operational performance of electricity distribution companies (DisCos) in Nigeria using quarterly data from the Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission (NERC) for 2025.

The analysis examines electricity billing, revenue collection, collection efficiency, customer metering, ATC&C losses, and customer complaints. It uses data analytics and interactive dashboards to identify quarterly trends and differences in performance across distribution companies.

## Research Topic

**Analysis of Financial and Operational Performance of Electricity Distribution Companies in Nigeria: Evidence from 2025 Quarterly NERC Data**

## Research Aim

To analyse the financial and operational performance of electricity distribution companies in Nigeria using quarterly NERC data for 2025.

## Research Objectives

1. To analyse the quarterly billing and revenue collection performance of electricity distribution companies in Nigeria.
2. To assess collection efficiency across the electricity distribution companies.
3. To examine metering performance and ATC&C losses across the distribution companies.
4. To analyse customer complaints reported by the distribution companies.
5. To compare financial and operational performance across the four quarters of 2025 and the distribution companies.

## Research Questions

1. How did total billings and revenue collected vary across the four quarters of 2025?
2. What differences exist in collection efficiency among electricity distribution companies?
3. How did metering rates and ATC&C losses vary across the distribution companies?
4. What trends and differences exist in customer complaints across the four quarters?
5. How did financial and operational performance differ among the distribution companies?

## Dataset and Scope

The project uses quarterly electricity sector data for 2025 extracted from NERC reports.

The dataset covers the following distribution companies:

- Aba
- Abuja
- Benin
- Eko
- Enugu
- Ibadan
- Ikeja
- Jos
- Kaduna
- Kano
- Port Harcourt
- Yola

**Key indicators include:**

- Total Billings (NGN Billion)
- Revenue Collected (NGN Billion)
- Collection Efficiency (%)
- Registered Customers
- Metered Customers
- Metering Rate (%)
- Customer Complaints Received
- ATC&C Loss (%)

The analysis covers four quarters: Q1, Q2, Q3 and Q4 2025.

## Methodology

The project follows a quantitative, descriptive data analytics approach.

The workflow includes:

1. Extracting relevant tables from quarterly NERC reports.
2. Consolidating the quarterly data into a structured dataset.
3. Cleaning and validating the extracted data.
4. Performing exploratory and comparative analysis.
5. Developing interactive dashboards to communicate key findings.

Missing values are retained where the original reports do not provide data. They are not automatically treated as zero.

## Dashboard

The interactive dashboard presents the analysis through the following sections:

### 01 — Executive Overview
Provides a high-level summary of financial and operational performance, including key indicators and comparisons.

### 02 — Quarterly Performance Analysis
Examines changes in billing, revenue collection, collection efficiency and other available indicators across the four quarters.

### 03 — DisCo Performance Comparison
Compares financial and operational indicators across the electricity distribution companies.

## Limitations

- The analysis is limited to the available 2025 quarterly data.
- Missing observations in the original reports are retained as missing.
- Customer complaints are treated as a reported indicator of customer-related issues, not as a direct measure of customer satisfaction.
- The analysis identifies descriptive patterns and differences; it does not establish causal relationships.
