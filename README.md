# Nigeria Electricity Sector Performance Dashboard (2025)

## Research Title

**Analysis of Financial and Operational Performance of Electricity Distribution Companies in Nigeria: Evidence from 2025 Quarterly NERC Data**

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyses the financial and operational performance of Nigeria's Electricity Distribution Companies (DisCos) using quarterly data for 2025 published by the Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission (NERC).

The project examines revenue collection, billing performance, collection efficiency, customer metering, Aggregate Technical, Commercial and Collection (ATC&C) losses, and customer complaints.

An interactive dashboard was developed using Looker Studio to present the indicators, compare performance across DisCos, and examine quarterly trends.

## 2. Problem Statement

Electricity distribution companies in Nigeria face challenges relating to revenue collection, metering coverage, distribution losses and customer complaints.

Understanding how these indicators vary across quarters and distribution companies can provide useful evidence for evaluating electricity distribution performance.

This project uses NERC quarterly data to examine these financial and operational indicators.

## 3. Research Objectives

1. To analyse the quarterly billing and revenue collection performance of electricity distribution companies in Nigeria.
2. To assess collection efficiency across the electricity distribution companies.
3. To examine metering performance and ATC&C losses across the distribution companies.
4. To analyse customer complaints reported by the distribution companies.
5. To compare financial and operational performance across the four quarters of 2025 and the distribution companies.

## 4. Research Questions

1. How did total billings and revenue collected vary across the four quarters of 2025?
2. What differences exist in collection efficiency among electricity distribution companies?
3. How did metering rates and ATC&C losses vary across the distribution companies?
4. What trends and differences exist in customer complaints across the four quarters?
5. How did financial and operational performance differ among the distribution companies?

## 5. Scope of the Study

The analysis covers the 2025 quarterly performance of Nigeria's electricity distribution companies.

The indicators include:

- Total Billings (NGN Billion)
- Revenue Collected (NGN Billion)
- Collection Efficiency (%)
- Registered Customers
- Metered Customers
- Metering Rate (%)
- Customer Complaints Received
- ATC&C Loss (%)

The study focuses on quarterly and company-level comparisons.

## 6. Data Source and Methodology

**Primary data source:** Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission (NERC) quarterly reports for 2025.

**Data preparation:** Data extraction, cleaning, validation and structuring into a consolidated dataset.

**Analytical approach:**
- Descriptive analysis of financial and operational indicators.
- Quarterly trend analysis.
- Comparative analysis across electricity distribution companies.
- Dashboard-based visualization and interactive filtering.

Missing values are retained where the corresponding information is not reported in the source data.

## 7. Dashboard Pages

### Page 2 — Quarterly Performance Analysis
